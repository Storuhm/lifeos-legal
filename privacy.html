LifeOS Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 August 2026

LifeOS is a personal, non-commercial application used by its owner for personal financial administration and life management.

Bank data

LifeOS may access bank account information through Enable Banking after explicit authorization by the account holder.

Bank data may include:

  • bank account information;
  • account balances;
  • booked transactions;
  • pending transaction information where available;
  • transaction references and counterparty information.

Purpose

Bank data is used exclusively for the account holder's personal financial administration within LifeOS, including transaction processing, categorization, recurring expense tracking and financial overview.

Storage

LifeOS processes bank synchronization through the user's personal Mac server. Provider credentials and private cryptographic keys are stored locally on that Mac using secure system storage and are not included in normal LifeOS data synchronization.

Sharing

LifeOS does not sell bank data and does not share it for advertising or marketing purposes. Bank data is only shared with service providers where technically necessary to provide the bank connection requested by the user.

Control and deletion

The account holder controls the LifeOS installation and may disconnect bank synchronization or delete locally stored LifeOS data.

Contact

Questions regarding this privacy policy can be sent to the contact email address registered for the LifeOS Enable Banking application.
